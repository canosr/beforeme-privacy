Privacy policy

What you save, what you share, and the choices that stay yours.

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Your account & saved information

BeforeMe uses email and password sign-in through Supabase Authentication. Your account identity, profile, rhythms, schedules, completions, reflections, and feature settings are stored in the app’s hosted Supabase service, not a database you need to supply or manage.

The app also keeps a sign-in session and local preferences on your device so you can stay signed in and keep your chosen setup. These records support sign-in, saved progress, synchronization, and the features you choose to use.

Calendars are optional context

With calendar permission, BeforeMe reads eligible events from calendars you select. Copies stored in hosted Supabase include event titles, start and end times, location, notes (including event URLs and calendar names), all-day and cancellation status, and event, series, calendar, and app-installation identifiers used for sync.

Calendar events are context, not tasks. They do not automatically create rhythms or checkoffs. You choose whether to link your own rhythm to an event. Linked rhythms support paired Before and After reminders, not a During reminder, subject to your reminder preferences, device notification permissions, and scheduling limits. Delivery is not guaranteed.

You can change selected calendars in Settings or revoke calendar permission in your device settings. Stopping access is not the same as deleting previously saved copies. Contact support if you need help removing saved calendar information.

Reflections & gratitude photos

Personal reflections and gratitude entries are account-owned information. Photos you choose to attach are uploaded to a private Supabase storage bucket; the app uses temporary signed URLs to display them. They are not automatically posted to Community or sent to friends. Avoid forwarding a signed photo link, because someone with that link may view it until it expires.

What you deliberately share in Community

Community is optional. When you join and publish a post or comment, that content and your displayed author identity are visible to other Community members. Reactions are also part of Community activity. Treat posts, prayer requests, gratitude, and comments as public within Community, not as private journal entries.

You can delete your own posts and comments using Community controls. Leaving Community does not itself delete previously published content. Other people may already have seen or copied what you shared.

Friends & Check-In privacy

Check-In stores your chosen username, first name, time zone, friend requests, connections, and check-in activity. Accepted friends can see your displayed identity, encouragements, and shared progress according to the connection’s privacy settings. Progress is shared as counts and completion activity, not your private rhythm titles, calendar details, or reflections.

Daily progress sharing is enabled by default for accepted friends; review each friend’s sharing controls. Completion alerts have a separate setting and are off by default. Optional friend discovery has its own opt-in and is off by default. If you enable it, other signed-in users can search your username and first name. You can change sharing or remove a friend without publishing anything to Community.

Device permissions & reminders

Calendar access, photo selection, and notifications support the features you choose. You can manage device permissions in system settings. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder content may appear on a lock screen according to your device’s notification settings, so choose those settings with your privacy in mind.

If you use iOS widgets, the app writes a snapshot of rhythm titles, categories, times, completion status and counts, and a verse to shared on-device storage for the widget. Widget content can be visible to people who can see your Home Screen or other widget surfaces. This snapshot does not include imported calendar event details or private reflections.

Deleting information, resetting & account deletion

Deleting an individual entry removes that entry through its feature’s controls. A reset is different: it clears the app data described in the reset confirmation and preserves your account and profile. A reset also removes your Community membership and content and your friend connections and activity. Signing out or uninstalling is not account deletion and does not by itself erase hosted data.

To permanently delete your account in the app, open Settings → Delete account. Type DELETE, tap Review account deletion, and confirm Delete Account Permanently. This removes your sign-in account and profile, saved rhythms and schedules, completions and history, imported calendar copies and links, reflections and gratitude photos, your Community membership and authored posts, comments and reactions, and connections and activity with friends. This cannot be undone. It does not delete events from your device’s original calendars or copies other people have already made of shared content.

A minimal deletion marker (tombstone) containing your account UUID and a creation timestamp is permanently retained to prevent old sessions or delayed writes from recreating deleted account data. This marker is not your journal or saved content. Service backups may retain copies under the hosting provider’s backup practices; we do not promise a fixed backup-retention or removal period.

If you cannot access your account or need help removing specific saved information, email williambetsyt@gmail.com. Never send your password. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

Questions & support messages

Email williambetsyt@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Opening the support email action does not automatically attach your account, calendar, journal, photos, diagnostic logs, or credentials. Only send details you choose to include. Your email provider handles the message you send.

Our public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening these links sends standard web-request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to GitHub as the hosting provider. Hosting logs and retention are governed by that provider’s practices; we do not promise a fixed retention period.
