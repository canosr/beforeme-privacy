The BeforeMe App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

BeforeMe collects the following information that you provide:

  • Your Apple account email address (used to sign you in)
  • Your display name (if you provide one)
  • Your rhythms, reflections, completions, and app settings (stored in your personal Supabase account)
  • The calendar events you choose to sync (stored in your personal Supabase account)
  • Your device calendar permissions (only the calendars you choose to allow)

BeforeMe does not collect your full calendar. You choose which calendars to sync, and only the events you choose are copied to your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Sign you in securely
  • Store your rhythms, reflections, and completions
  • Sync the calendar events you choose so you can connect rhythms to them
  • Send intentional reminders before, during, or after linked calendar events
  • Improve the app for you

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With Apple, as necessary to provide the app through the App Store and TestFlight
  • With Supabase (the authentication and database service we use) strictly to store and serve your account data

Your rhythms, reflections, and calendar events are stored in your own Supabase account and are not shared with other users.

4. Data Security

Your data is stored in Supabase, which uses encryption in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard authentication and access controls. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Sign out of BeforeMe at any time
  • Remove your rhythms and reflections from within the app
  • Contact us to request deletion of your personal data

To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

BeforeMe is family-friendly and may be used by minors under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy at this same URL.
