Support

A question, a problem, or a privacy request? We’re here to help.

Last updated: 2026-10-17

How can we help?

Email williambetsyt@gmail.com for app support or privacy questions. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and BeforeMe version/build if useful. You can find the app version in Settings → Help & Support.

Describe what happened and what you expected. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, private calendar details, journal entries, or sensitive photos. No account data or diagnostic logs are attached automatically. Email is handled by your chosen email provider.

Delete your account

In BeforeMe, open Settings → Delete account. Read the permanent-deletion notice, type DELETE, choose Review account deletion, and confirm only if you want to permanently delete your account and its saved data. Cancel leaves your account intact.

This removes your account, rhythms, reflections, gratitude photos, published Community content, and friend connections. Reset also removes Community content and friend connections along with the saved app data described in the reset flow, but preserves your sign-in account and profile; signing out or uninstalling does not delete hosted information.

If you cannot access the app or need help removing specific information, email support. We may need to verify ownership; never send your password. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

Common questions

Calendar events: calendars are optional context, not tasks. Choose calendars in Calendar settings. You can revoke access in iOS Settings; stopping sync does not itself erase previously saved copies.

Reminders: review BeforeMe’s reminder preferences and iOS notification permissions, including lock-screen previews.

Friends and Community: review each friend’s sharing controls. Community posts are shared with other members; keep private reflections in your journal.

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; page requests are handled by that hosting service.
